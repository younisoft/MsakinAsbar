Privacy policy

We respect the privacy of our clients and residents. This policy explains what data we collect when you use the Aspar Residences website or contract our services, how we use and protect it, in line with the Personal Data Protection Law of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.

Last updated: 14 November 2026
Aspar Residences services are provided by شركة أسبار الموحدة التجارية (Aspar Unified Trading Company) · Commercial Registration No. 4030407410 · Unified No. 7021495051

Data we collect

We only collect the data needed to provide our services, including:

  • Contact details: name, company name, mobile number and email.
  • Request details: city or work site, headcount, services needed, length of stay and start date.
  • Resident details once contracted: names, ID or iqama numbers and what regulations require for housing registration, supplied by the contracting company.
  • Billing details: company details, commercial registration, VAT number and bank transfer information.

How the website request form works

When you fill in the quote form, a message containing your entries is prepared and opened in WhatsApp for you to review and send yourself. The website does not store this data on its servers. Once sent, the message is subject to WhatsApp’s privacy policy.

How we use data

We use data to:

  • Prepare quotes and contact you about your request.
  • Deliver the contract: housing, transport, catering and facilities management.
  • Issue invoices and collect payments.
  • Keep residents and facilities safe and follow up on issues and maintenance.
  • Comply with laws and requirements of the competent authorities.

Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell or rent personal data. We may share it only as far as necessary with:

  • Service providers working on our behalf, such as transport, catering, maintenance and billing systems, who are bound to protect it.
  • Government and regulatory authorities where the law requires.
  • Another party in a merger, acquisition or asset transfer, with data protection continuing to apply.

Data security

We apply reasonable technical and administrative measures to protect data from unauthorised access or use, and limit access to the staff concerned. No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, however.

Data retention

We keep data for as long as we provide the service and for any period the law requires afterwards, such as accounting and tax requirements. We then delete it securely or anonymise it.

Your rights

Under the Personal Data Protection Law you have the right to:

  • Be informed of how your data is collected and used.
  • Access your data and obtain a copy of it.
  • Ask us to correct or complete inaccurate data.
  • Ask us to destroy your data once it is no longer needed, unless the law requires us to keep it.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

Cookies

Our website does not currently use tracking or advertising cookies. If we add analytics or marketing tools in future, we will update this policy before using them.

Third-party links and services

The website may link to external services such as WhatsApp or maps. We are not responsible for their privacy practices and recommend reviewing their policies.

Minors’ privacy

Our services are for companies and adult workers. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18, and will delete it if we learn we have.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We publish the updated version on this page with its date, and notify contracted clients of material changes by email or other agreed channels.
