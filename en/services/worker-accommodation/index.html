Worker accommodation for companies

A place worthy of your team. We provide worker accommodation for companies, with furnished rooms and ongoing operations, and arrange services around your project location, headcount and length of stay.

Plan your team’s needs →

Housing that fits your team’s day

Choosing accommodation starts with the nature of the work. Project location, shift times and length of stay all shape what you need. Share these details and we will review the right options and furnishings before operations are agreed.

What does worker accommodation include?

Rooms come furnished with beds and lockers, with shared facilities. Furnishings and services vary by site and package, so we set out exactly what the offer includes, and what needs extra arrangement, before you sign.

Housing, transport and catering in one request

You can request accommodation on its own or add transport, catering and facilities management. When services are combined, housing needs, trip times and meals sit within one agreed plan, with a single point of contact for follow-up.

Cleaning and maintenance during the stay

Ready rooms and facilities are part of the housing experience. The offer defines the scope of cleaning, maintenance and operational follow-up, and how issues are reported and tracked through to resolution.

How to get an accommodation quote

Prepare your headcount, city or work site, length of stay and preferred start date. Add any transport or catering needs and room requirements. This helps our team prepare a clearer offer, without assuming every site has the same furnishings or cost.

Questions about worker accommodation for companies

Can I book accommodation without extra services?

Yes. You can choose accommodation alone or add transport, catering and facilities management. The quote sets out the details.

Is the cost of worker accommodation fixed?

The offer depends on location, headcount, length of stay, furnishings and services. Contact us to review your needs.
