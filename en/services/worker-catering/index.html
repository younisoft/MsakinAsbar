Worker catering

Care in every meal. We arrange catering for your team at times that suit the project, with attention to food safety and a clear scope of service from the start.

Plan your team’s needs →

Meals within a clear working day

A catering plan starts with headcount and working hours. Meal times, where meals are served and how many are needed per day help us define the right service. Share your actual needs so the offer reflects your project.

Variety that suits the team

We provide varied meals according to the agreed plan. If your team has preferences or special dietary requirements, mention them when you request a quote so we can discuss them and set them out in the offer before service begins.

Meals coordinated with shifts

Meal times can differ between teams. Setting out headcount and timing for each shift helps organize catering around the team’s day, especially when accommodation and transport are part of the same service.

Food safety and follow-up

Attention to food safety is part of the service. We review how meals are served and how issues are raised, and the agreement defines the scope of service and operating responsibilities rather than relying on a general package description.

Requesting a catering quote

Prepare your headcount, number of daily meals, service location, shift times and operating period. Mention any dietary requirements from the start. Details are reviewed before the offer is approved, and scope may vary by site and agreed needs.

Questions about worker catering

Can meal times be scheduled?

We review your project hours and shifts, then arrange meals according to the agreed plan.

Does an accommodation package include catering automatically?

Catering can be added to an accommodation request. The quote clearly sets out the services included.
