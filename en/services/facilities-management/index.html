Worker accommodation facilities management

We take care of the details. We handle cleaning, maintenance, supervision and operational follow-up to keep accommodation facilities ready, within the agreed scope.

Plan your team’s needs →

Management that starts with knowing the site

Every residence has its own details: rooms, corridors and shared facilities. We review the site and the services needed before agreeing the scope of facilities management, so tasks are clear to your team and ours.

Cleaning in rooms and facilities

Defining the areas covered and working hours helps organize cleaning. Service details are set out in the offer by site and package, including shared facilities and anything that needs special arrangement.

Maintenance and issue tracking

Reporting an issue is where follow-up begins. We agree how residence issues are received and tracked to resolution, and define maintenance responsibilities in the agreement. Any equipment or works needing extra arrangements should be clarified before operations start.

Supervision through one point of contact

Instead of spreading housing details across several providers, accommodation, facilities management and related services can be combined in one request. We set out the scope of supervision, follow-up and reporting in the agreed plan, so you know who to contact and what the service covers.

How to define the scope of facilities management

Share the location, headcount and a description of rooms and facilities, and set out your cleaning, maintenance and supervision needs. Add any current operational issues and the preferred start date. Our team then discusses the details with you before preparing the offer and approving the scope of work.

Questions about worker accommodation facilities management

Does the service include cleaning and maintenance?

Yes. The service covers cleaning, maintenance and supervision according to the site scope and agreed package, with details in the offer.

How are residence issues tracked?

We agree with you how issues are reported and tracked to resolution within the scope of service.
