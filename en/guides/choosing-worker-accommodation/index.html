Guide to choosing worker accommodation

Housing is a decision that shapes your team’s day. Before comparing prices, compare what the service includes and how well it fits the work site and project schedule.

Plan your team’s needs →

Start with location and working hours

Write down the project location and shift times first. Review the route between accommodation and work and discuss travel times with the provider. Distance on a map alone does not show access details or daily transport needs.

Check the room furnishings yourself

Ask for a clear description of rooms, beds, lockers, bathrooms and shared facilities. Furnishings vary between sites and packages, so do not rely on a single photo or short description. Ask what is actually available at the proposed site for your team.

Compare the full cost of service

When comparing two offers, review what each includes: accommodation, transport, catering, cleaning and maintenance. Ask about extra items and the terms for changing headcount or duration. Comparison is clearer when the scope is similar and written down.

Define responsibility for cleaning and maintenance

Ask which areas are cleaned, how maintenance issues are reported and who handles follow-up. It helps to agree how communication works before the team moves in, rather than leaving these details until the first issue.

Coordinate catering with shifts

If you need meals, specify the number, timing and where they are served. Mention any special dietary requirements for review. Make sure meal details are written into the offer, especially if your teams work different hours.

Get the scope in writing before you agree

Put headcount, length of stay, start date and services needed into one request. Review the offer with your team and ask for clarification on anything undefined. This checklist helps you discuss operational needs; site requirements, permits and each party’s responsibilities still need independent verification before contracting.

Questions about choosing accommodation

What information do I need to request an accommodation quote?

Headcount, city or work site, length of stay, start date and the services you need.

How do I compare worker accommodation offers?

Compare scope of services, furnishings, location and service duration first, then review cost and extra items on a like-for-like basis.
